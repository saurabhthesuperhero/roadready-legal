Privacy Policy: Road Ready

Last updated: 18 November 2026

DMV Practice Test: Road Ready ("the app", package com.saikaizen.roadready) is a study app for the US learner's permit knowledge test, published by Saikaizen Innovations ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information is handled when you use it. The app is not affiliated with any government agency.

Information we do not collect

The app has no account and no sign-in. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, birth date, address, contacts, photos or precise location, and we run no servers of our own for the app.

Information stored only on your phone

Your state, licence type, test date, daily goal, the answers you give, mistakes, bookmarks, streak, settings and your age group (under 13, 13 to 17, or 18 or older, as you choose it; no birth date) are stored only on your device. We never receive them.

Analytics

To understand how the app is used and to improve it (for example, which topics learners find hard), the app uses Google Analytics for Firebase. It records usage events such as screens opened, practice rounds and exam simulators started and finished, topics and state chosen, reminders switched on or off, ads shown, and purchases made (the product, not your payment details), together with device information (model, Android version, language, country), an app instance identifier, the advertising identifier and approximate location derived from your IP address. This data is not linked to your name or any account and is processed by Google under the Firebase privacy terms. Google signals and ads personalisation are turned off for this app's analytics. Detailed event data is kept for 2 months; only aggregated reports are kept longer. If you tell the app you are under 13, no analytics events are sent.

Advertising

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob: a banner on practice and review screens, a rectangle on the results screen, an occasional full-screen ad after you close a result (never during a test), and videos you choose to watch to unlock a reward. There are no ads when the app opens. To show and measure ads, AdMob may collect and process your device's advertising identifier, approximate location (derived from your IP address), device information and how you interact with ads, and may use cookies or similar technologies. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

  • Under 13: no ads of any kind are shown.
  • 13 to 17, or age not answered: only non-personalised ads, with Google's teen (or child) ad protections switched on and a limited ad content rating.
  • 18 or older: where required (for example in the EEA, the UK and some US states) the app asks for your choice through Google's consent form before personalised ads are shown. You can change it any time in Settings, Ad privacy choices.

You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings. Buying Pro removes all ads.

In-app purchases

Pro is a one-time purchase processed by Google Play Billing. We never receive or store your payment details. Your purchase is checked on your device so that Pro is granted and can be restored after a reinstall.

Notifications

If you switch on the daily reminder, the app asks for notification permission and schedules the reminder on your device. No data is sent for this. You can turn it off in Settings or in Android settings.

Links and sharing

Handbook links open your state's official website in your browser; that website's own privacy policy applies. If you share a result card, the app opens your device's share sheet; what you share and with whom is up to you, and we do not receive it.

Children

The app is made for learner drivers aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. If a user says they are under 13, the app shows no ads and sends no analytics. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has used the app, contact us at the address below.

Your choices, retention and deletion

Settings, Erase my progress, clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app deletes everything stored on your phone. Data collected by Google for analytics and advertising is handled under Google's Privacy Policy; you can reset your advertising ID at any time. Depending on where you live (for example California or another US state with a privacy law, the EEA or the UK) you may have rights to access, delete or opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information; because we hold no data that identifies you, the controls above are the way to exercise them, and you can email us with any request.

Security

Data exchanged with the analytics, advertising and billing services is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.
